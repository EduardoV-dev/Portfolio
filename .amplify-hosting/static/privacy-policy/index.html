Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 04, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Eduardo Varela (“I”, “me”, or “my”) handles information in connection with this portfolio website (eduardov-dev.com). I take privacy seriously and aim to be transparent about the minimal data practices in place.

Information I Collect

This site does not collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information (PII) directly. There are no user accounts, sign-up forms, or databases connected to this website.

If you choose to contact me via the contact form or directly by email, your name and email address will be used solely to respond to your inquiry and will not be shared with any third party.

Analytics

This site may use anonymous, aggregate analytics (such as page views and referrer data) to understand general traffic patterns. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics. Data is not sold or shared with advertisers.

Cookies

This site does not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. Any cookies present are strictly necessary for site functionality (e.g. theme preference) and do not track individuals across websites.

Third-Party Services

This site is hosted on AWS CloudFront / S3. AWS may collect standard server log data (IP addresses, request timestamps, user agents) as part of normal CDN operation. This data is governed by AWS’s Privacy Policy .

Any embedded fonts (e.g. Google Fonts, if used) may result in requests to third-party servers. Where possible, fonts are self-hosted to avoid this.

Data Retention

As this site does not store personal data, there is no retention policy applicable beyond what is described in Third-Party Services above. Contact emails are retained only as long as needed to address the inquiry.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding access, correction, or deletion of personal data. Since this site collects essentially no personal data, most requests will be trivially fulfilled. For any privacy-related inquiries, use the contact form on the contact page.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy occasionally. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
